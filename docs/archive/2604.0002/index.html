Country regulatory landscape

[Source: section “07 · PROPOSALS OF RECORD” of the original page, table; the columns “Submitted” and “Status” are not reproduced.]

Country Recipient · Framework · National context
🇺🇿 Uzbekistan Ministry of Digital Development · Buyruq 3787 · PQ-358 · Strategy "Raqamli O'zbekiston 2030" · $1B AI investment, 100+ government AI projects, no quality measurement instrument. First-mover position in CIS.
🇦🇪 UAE MBZUAI · TDRA · DSI · AI Strategy 2031 · $169B invested in AI infrastructure. Falcon built, MBZUAI launched. The measurement standard layer is missing.
🇹🇷 Turkey Digital Transformation Office · BTK · AI Bill 2024 · First country in history to ban an AI system (Grok, July 2025). Trustworthy AI Seal proposed but not built. Bridge position between EU and Middle East.
🇸🇦 Saudi Arabia SDAIA · National AI Strategy 2030 · $40B+ AI investment program. Quality-control infrastructure for portfolio-wide measurement.
🇸🇬 Singapore IMDA · Model AI Governance Framework · AI Verify (voluntary toolkit). Mature governance infrastructure that requires a quantitative enforcement layer.
🇰🇷 South Korea MSIT · AI Basic Act in force · Companies require certification clarity. K-AI national quality designation opportunity.
🇯🇵 Japan METI · AI Guidelines · AI Basic Act pending · Guidelines without measurement teeth. Path to enforceable national quality standard.
🇩🇪 Germany BSI · EU AI Act implementation · €17B/yr EU compliance spending. First EU member state to deploy operational AI measurement instrument.
🇺🇸 USA EO 14110 (rescinded January 2025) · NIST AI RMF (voluntary) · State patchwork (CA SB-1047 vetoed, CO AI Act 2026, NYC AEDT). Largest AI economy without federal enforcement instrument.
🇨🇳 China CAC · Generative AI Measures (2023) · Algorithm Recommendation Provisions · Deep Synthesis Provisions. Mature regulatory framework requiring cross-border verification layer for international compatibility.
🇬🇧 United Kingdom AISI · pro-innovation approach (no horizontal AI law) · sectoral regulators. Principles without measurement; ONTO converts principles into auditable scores.
🇮🇳 India MeitY · IndiaAI Mission ($1.25B) · sectoral approach. National sovereign AI buildout requires deployment-stage quality measurement.
🇫🇷 France DGE · EU AI Act · sovereign-AI push (Mistral, national cloud). National measurement layer for sovereign-AI ecosystem.
🇧🇷 Brazil PL 2338/2023 AI Bill in legislature · LGPD precedent. Latin America first-mover position on enforceable AI quality standard.
