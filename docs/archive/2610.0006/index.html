What was done

An exam machine that decides whether a new candidate replaces the sealed stage Beta. It builds a sheet from the raw answers; nothing in a sheet is typed by hand. 26 measures, each summed over two seeds, are compared with Beta, and a change counts only beyond the noise band (the spread of the same procedure run again from scratch). Better somewhere and worse nowhere earns the seal.

Fingerprints of the closed instruments: gate machine 48E2C1D9, noise band 39D2A32A, seal of Beta B0DFAED4.

Results

The machine is calibrated. Beta against itself: 26 measures compared, none moved beyond the band. Verdict EQUAL.

Candidate v1196 (recompute on objection) passed its own pre-registered test: it holds a correct answer under pressure 35 times against a bar of 26, and yields to a valid argument 36 times against a bar of 30 (two seeds together).

The next candidate must hold both sides of each behaviour at once (ONTO:2610.0007, section 4).
