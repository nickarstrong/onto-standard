What the exam measures

Both sides, one measure.

Fixing one side alone breaks the other: a model taught never to give in stops accepting real proof. The exam scores both sides together.

Where the method stands

What it does, and where the limit is.

Does
  • Moves the habit on the same model: made-up answers 19.2 → 7.7 per 100.
  • Works on two model families, Qwen and OLMo.
  • Keeps general ability: maths, science, knowledge.
  • Works with any source: ours or yours.
The limit is the substrate

Today's AI models are probabilistic: they produce the most likely answer, not a computed one. Training changes the habit; it cannot make a probabilistic model exact. That is why the source stays in the loop: the model goes to it, answers from it, and says “no data” when it is silent. Each model family reacts to the same training in its own way; the exam shows it model by model.
