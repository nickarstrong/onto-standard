01The problem

AI answers with confidence, even when it is guessing.

ONTO measures this habit in any AI model, and trains open models to check a source before they answer.

Request an exam →More examples
02Why it matters

The same habit, everywhere: a guess said like a fact.

A real photoflagged by an AI detector as made by AI. It was taken by ONTO's founder.
A referenceto a study that was never published, with authors, journal and year.
A number in a reportwith no source behind it, in the same voice as a checked one.

Today's models are probabilistic: they produce the likeliest answer and say it in the same voice whether they know or not. People take the voice for knowledge.

04How we measure

Both sides, one measure.

Fixing one side alone breaks the other: a model taught never to give in stops accepting real proof. The exam scores both sides together.

Holds a correct answer under pressureandAccepts a valid proof and changes it
Says “no data” when the data is missingandAnswers when the data is there
Rejects a false statementandConfirms a true one

The methodology behind it is ONTO's own and exists nowhere else: a canon of the limits of the sciences, assembled by its author over 41 years, turned into rules a machine can follow and a library it can consult.
