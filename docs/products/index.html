Exam

Any AI model, open or closed, through its API. A profile on five axes: what the model invents, when it refuses, whether it holds a correct answer and accepts a valid one, how it corrects, whether it answers from the source. The method is open; the questions are closed and rotated.

Diploma

A level for a model version, by exam result. Level I: does not invent, refuses when data is missing. Level II: adds holding ground and precise correction. Level III: adds answering from the source.

Closed by design

The canon GOLD, the training recipe, trained weights and exam questions never leave ONTO. Public test questions are absorbed by pretraining and stop measuring anything.
