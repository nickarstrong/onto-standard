ONTO · About

An independent institute for epistemic discipline in AI.

ONTO keeps a measurable standard, examines models against it and trains the discipline into the weights of open models. We do not run a chat product and do not sell a model. Founded in 2024 by Khakim Angenoev; the path is in How ONTO became an institute.

Trust
  • People who write exam questions do not train models.
  • Thresholds are written before the data.
  • The price of an exam does not depend on its result.
  • Partnerships are non-exclusive, with no NDA on the method and no change of criteria on request.
Not there yet

What we do not have yet.

The exam for any model opens with v0.9 in Q1 2027. No outside replication has been done so far. On questions written by people outside ONTO, our student still invents 7.7 answers in 100 against a goal of 5.
